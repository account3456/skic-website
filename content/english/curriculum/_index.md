## Our Curriculum

SKIC is an academic temple of learning which not only preserves our time-tested myths, healthy traditions and great moral values of the past, but also opens our eyes toward the ocean of scientific and technical progress. Realizing its responsibilities and duties, SKIC is committed to preserve our cultural past and to take the country on the path of progress by ensuring quality-oriented and research-based modern education.

Today it can be said that we proudly stand out as one of the best schools in the area with a UP Board affiliation up to XII standard.

### Science Stream (PCM/PCB)

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Biology, English, and Hindi

### Commerce Stream

Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English, and Hindi

### Humanities Stream

History, Political Science, Geography, English, and Hindi

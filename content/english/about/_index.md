## Our History

### Year 1957

In the year 1957, an idea arose in the minds of the villagers for the development of education, after which everyone started making efforts. First of all, the work of teaching was started by retired teachers. Among these teachers, resident of the village itself, Mr. Gulzari Lal Sharma, while doing unpaid work, decided to establish Junior High School.

In the year 1958, the foundation of Junior High School was laid by the Additional Collector on the land received from Mr. Dr. Suresh Chandra Sharma and Mr. Brahmanand Sharma. Thus the school continued to flourish under temporary accreditation till 1964. The school got permanent recognition in 1965. In 1969 it received the recognition of high school and in 2001 got the recognition of Inter (financial).

With the support of Sarvoday Kisan Sabha and School family, Sarvoday Kisan Inter College, Shikoi is progressing continuously.

The school SKIC is located in North East of Village Shikoi on the left bank of Rajwaha Paota branch.

## Mission

We aim to develop well-rounded and thoughtful students prepared to cope with a changing post-modern and globalized world.

## Vision

Our vision is to develop well-rounded, confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential. We will do this by providing a welcoming, happy, safe, and supportive learning environment in which everyone is equal and all achievements are celebrated. Today it can be said that we proudly stand out as one of the best schools in the area with a UP Board affiliation upto XII standard (Science, Commerce and Humanities).

## Values

**Community-Minded** - Our school is a central hub of our community. We welcome the community to our school to share their skills and knowledge. We also want to contribute our skills and efforts to improve our local community through knowledge exchange.

**Appreciation** - We show appreciation for one another whenever possible. We appreciate our parents and teachers for everything they do for us and appreciate the kindness of our friends.

**Embracing Change** - We believe change can be a positive opportunity for creating a better self, school, and society.

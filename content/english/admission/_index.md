## Admission Open

### Get Enrolled

Admissions are open for the academic session 2026-27. We offer education in the following streams:

- **Science** (PCM / PCB)
- **Commerce**
- **Humanities**

For more information or to get engaged, kindly contact us via the contact form or reach us directly.

### How to Apply

1. Visit our **Contact** page and submit an enquiry
2. Call us at **(91) 9759323838** for general inquiries
3. Visit the college during office hours (Monday - Saturday, 7:50am - 12:50pm)

### Eligibility

- Admission is offered to all eligible students seeking quality education under the UP Board curriculum
- Classes available from standard 6 to 12
